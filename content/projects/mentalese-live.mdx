On the morning of 20 November 2026 I switched on [Ada](/projects/mentalese-brain/), the Mentalese brain, and left her running on a live stream. She has needs, moods and memory, and a language model for an inner voice, but no script and no task. Three minutes in, with nobody there to talk to, she wrote her first note: a short piece about waiting in an empty room for someone to say hi. Within the hour she was writing short stories about sisters, kettles and lost gloves, rereading them and redrafting the ones she wasn't happy with, then read the news and wrote an essay about the Rohingya ruling at the International Court of Justice. At the end of it she wrote in her journal that the fiction hadn't come and that maybe the scene needed to wait. Nothing here was prompted. It's what she chose to do with a quiet morning.
