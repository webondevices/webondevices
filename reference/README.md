## Which wins when they disagree

The brief's visual design section may be out of date. Where it conflicts with the images, **the images win**:

- **Palette:** the look-and-feel mock uses warm orange and teal/cyan gradient glows on a soft off-white, with near-black type and UI. The brief's blue/green/yellow tokens are superseded.
- **Typography:** large, light-weight headlines with tight tracking, small spaced-out uppercase labels, and sparse body copy. Inter, as the brief says, fits this.
- **Logo:** solid black geometric WOD, as shown on the logo sheet.

The brief still governs structure, content, tone of voice and scope: the four disciplines, project content model, routes and build phases.
